01 / Journey & Story

Finance taught me how businesses work. Media taught me why people care.

I come from Somnath, Gujarat, a place that shaped my cultural grounding, values, and an early exposure to entrepreneurship through our family hospitality business. Currently, I am based in Mumbai to pursue my postgraduate studies.

I started my academic journey with a BBA (Hons.) in Finance at Marwadi University, gaining a deep understanding of balance sheets, market dynamics, and operational feasibility. To explore how ideas truly connect with human attention, I transitioned to a PGDM in Media & Entertainment at Welingkar Institute of Management (WeSchool), Mumbai.

My core passion lives at the intersection of marketing, advertising, media & entertainment, digital marketing and creative technology. I am fascinated by what moves a consumer from simply noticing a brand to actively taking part in it.
